Digital asset · BitcoinOpen, global networks
A new kind of value

So, what is crypto?

Cryptocurrency is a digital asset that can move across the internet using blockchain networks. These shared records let people send value and build services without relying on a single central ledger.

⌘
Open by designMany networks are available around the clock and can be accessed from anywhere online.
⇄
More than paymentsBlockchains can support digital ownership, programmable agreements and new financial tools.
◎
Still evolvingTechnology and regulation are changing. Understand the risks before making a decision.

Clarity and care

Confidence starts with the details

Good decisions need clear information, thoughtful safeguards and room to learn.

◈

Know what you’re using

Look for clear information about fees, custody, withdrawals and the service provider before you sign up.

⌑

Protect your account

Use a unique password, enable two step verification and never share your one time password or recovery phrase.

◎

Understand the risks

Crypto prices can rise and fall sharply. Consider your own circumstances and never trade money you cannot afford to lose.
